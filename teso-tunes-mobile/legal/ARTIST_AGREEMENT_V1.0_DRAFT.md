# TesoHub Music Artist Distribution and Streaming Agreement

**Version:** 1.0 DRAFT  
**Status:** Draft for founder and legal review. Not published and not in force.  
**Operator:** TesoHub Music  
**Website:** https://tesohubmusic.com

This draft has not been represented as legally reviewed. It must not be published or enforced until the founder has approved the final text and obtained any legal advice considered necessary.

## 1. Introduction

This Artist Distribution and Streaming Agreement (the "Agreement") explains the terms on which an artist may use TesoHub Music to create an artist profile, submit music and artwork, and make approved music available to listeners. "Artist" means the person or authorized representative using the artist account. "TesoHub Music" means the platform available through the TesoHub Music application and website.

## 2. Eligibility and registration

The Artist must provide accurate account and application information and must be legally able to enter this Agreement. A representative applying for a group, label, estate, or another person must have authority to act for the relevant rights holder. Application does not guarantee approval. TesoHub Music may request reasonable additional information before approving or continuing an artist account.

## 3. Ownership and intellectual property

The Artist keeps ownership of the music, recordings, compositions, artwork, name, image, and other material that the Artist owns. Uploading material does not transfer copyright ownership to TesoHub Music. This Agreement does not remove any rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

## 4. Limited licence to TesoHub Music

For material submitted to and approved by TesoHub Music, the Artist grants TesoHub Music a limited, non-exclusive, worldwide licence to store, reproduce for technical delivery, stream, display, distribute through the platform, and promote that material in connection with TesoHub Music. The licence allows normal technical processing such as encoding, resizing artwork, caching, previews, and making the approved material available on supported devices. The Artist may also distribute the same material elsewhere.

This licence continues while the material is available on TesoHub Music and for a reasonable technical period needed to complete an approved removal, preserve security backups, comply with law, resolve disputes, and maintain audit records.

## 5. Artist responsibilities and copyright authorization

The Artist confirms that the Artist owns or has obtained all permissions needed to upload and authorize use of each recording, composition, beat, sample, feature, performance, image, name, and other submitted material. The Artist must accurately identify collaborators and must not upload unlawful, infringing, misleading, impersonating, harmful, or unauthorized material. The Artist is responsible for agreements with producers, writers, performers, labels, publishers, collective management organizations, and other rights holders.

## 6. Music submission and approval

Music and artwork may be reviewed for ownership concerns, metadata, technical quality, safety, policy compliance, and suitability for the platform. TesoHub Music may approve, reject, request changes to, delay, hide, or remove a submission. Review does not mean that TesoHub Music guarantees ownership, originality, legal compliance, audience reach, or publication by a particular date.

## 7. Revenue, royalties, and future monetization

Basic artist registration and music uploads are initially free. No specific royalty percentage, payment amount, revenue share, minimum payment, or payment date is promised by this Agreement. The Artist has not agreed through this draft to waive royalties or other rights that may apply under copyright law or a separate agreement.

If TesoHub Music introduces advertising, subscriptions, sponsorship, paid distribution, royalty reporting, or another monetization system, any artist revenue-sharing terms must be documented and communicated before they apply. A separate acceptance or updated agreement may be required.

## 8. Artist promotion

TesoHub Music may use an approved artist name, profile, artwork, release metadata, and short music excerpts to feature and promote the Artist, the submitted music, and the TesoHub Music service. Promotion is not guaranteed and does not create an employment, partnership, agency, or exclusive relationship.

## 9. Music removal and takedown requests

The Artist may request removal of the Artist's music through the supported Artist Studio or Support process. The ordinary voluntary-removal process may use a 30-day notice period. TesoHub Music may act sooner for copyright complaints, court or government orders, security incidents, policy violations, fraudulent ownership claims, or other urgent legal or safety reasons. Removal from the public service does not require deletion of lawful audit, dispute, backup, or compliance records.

## 10. Account security

The Artist must protect login credentials, use the correct artist account, and promptly report suspected unauthorized access. The Artist is responsible for actions taken through the account until TesoHub Music is notified and can reasonably secure it. TesoHub Music staff should not ask the Artist to disclose a password.

## 11. Copyright complaints

Rights holders may report alleged infringement through TesoHub Music Support. The complainant should identify the protected work, the disputed material, the claimed rights, and reliable contact information. TesoHub Music may restrict content while reviewing a complaint and may request information from the Artist. False or abusive claims and knowingly unauthorized uploads may lead to account or content action.

## 12. Personal data and privacy

TesoHub Music may process account, application, verification, agreement-acceptance, upload, support, usage, security, and audit information to operate and protect the service. Private verification notes, security credentials, and unreleased audio are not intended for public listeners. Data may be retained when reasonably required for accounts, rights records, disputes, security, legal obligations, and platform recovery. Applicable privacy notices and Ugandan law also apply.

## 13. Updates to this Agreement

TesoHub Music may prepare a new version when the service, law, or business model changes. Published versions are preserved as immutable records. When renewed consent is required, the Artist must be shown the new version and accept it before the affected artist action can continue. A new version does not silently rewrite the version previously accepted.

## 14. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Uganda. The parties should first try in good faith to resolve a concern through TesoHub Music Support. Nothing in this section removes a right or remedy that applicable law does not permit the parties to exclude.

## 15. Electronic acceptance

By selecting the unchecked acceptance box and continuing through the authenticated TesoHub Music workflow, the Artist confirms that the Artist has read and agrees to the published version displayed at that time. TesoHub Music records the authenticated account, agreement version, content hash, and server-generated acceptance time as evidence of that electronic acceptance. No person at TesoHub Music may accept the Agreement on the Artist's behalf.
