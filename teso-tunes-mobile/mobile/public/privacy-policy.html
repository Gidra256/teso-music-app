Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, protect and manage information across the TesoHub Music website and mobile application.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TesoHub Music handles information when you use the TesoHub Music website, Android application and related services. It applies to listeners, artists and visitors. Our privacy contact is support@tesohubmusic.com.

2. Information we handle

The information involved depends on how you use the platform and which features you choose:

  • Account and contact information: details such as your email address, name or username, profile and information needed to sign in or recover access. Authentication credentials are processed as part of providing account access.
  • Artist submissions: artist names, biographies, photos, songs, artwork, application details, artist agreements and any verification materials requested for artist review.
  • Listening and account activity: likes, follows, playlists, account preferences and interactions with music and artists.
  • Support communications: messages, reports and information you provide when contacting our team or requesting deletion.
  • Technical information: information that can be processed when you connect to our services, such as IP address, browser or device information, error details, request logs and security events.

We encourage you not to submit unnecessary sensitive information when contacting support.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate and secure accounts, stream and organize music, maintain artist profiles and releases, process artist applications and agreements, offer likes, follows and playlists, troubleshoot technical issues, communicate with users, investigate misuse and respond to rights, support and privacy requests.

4. Public information and music

Once approved and published, artist profiles, names, biographies, artwork, song titles and audio releases may be publicly visible. A song or profile you provide for publication may be seen or shared by other listeners. Do not submit music, photographs or personal information unless you have the necessary rights and permission.

5. Service providers and sharing

We use hosting and technical infrastructure, including Render and Supabase, to operate our API, database and storage. Our support email service may also process communications you send us. These providers process data needed to provide their respective services. We may disclose information if required by applicable law or to address security, fraud or rights-related concerns. We will update this policy if our providers or data practices materially change.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect user information and limit unauthorized access. No service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong password and do not share your account credentials.

7. Retention and account deletion

We keep information as needed to provide the service, maintain account and artist records, respond to requests, investigate misuse and meet applicable legal obligations. When a verified account-deletion request is processed, we will remove or de-identify associated account information that is no longer required. This can include profile information, playlists, likes and follows. Artist release removal and rights-related records may require additional review to honor existing agreements and lawful obligations.

Some limited records may need to be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or legal reasons. Where retention applies, we will explain the category and reason in our response to the request.

To request account and associated data deletion, use the dedicated Account Deletion page. This page is available without signing in.

8. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about your personal information, correct inaccurate account details, or request account deletion. We may ask for reasonable account verification before making changes. Do not email us your password.

9. Children's privacy

TesoHub Music is a music platform. We will follow applicable age-related requirements for the audiences identified in our store listing and the laws that apply to users. Parents or guardians may contact us about privacy concerns involving a child.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as the platform changes. The latest version will be available here, with an updated date at the top.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions, data requests or concerns, email support@tesohubmusic.com.
