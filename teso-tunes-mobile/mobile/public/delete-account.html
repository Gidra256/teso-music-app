Request account and data deletion

If you have a TesoHub Music listener or artist account, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal information at any time. You do not need the app installed to start the request.

Step 1: Send an email from the address associated with your TesoHub Music account to support@tesohubmusic.com.
Step 2: Use the subject Account Deletion Request and include your username or artist name if applicable.
Step 3: Our team will verify account ownership if needed, review associated data and confirm the outcome.
Email account deletion request ↗

Prefer to request from the app? When signed in, open Settings → Privacy & Data → Request account deletion and submit a support request.

Data covered by the request

A verified deletion request covers the user account and related data that is no longer required for legitimate purposes. Depending on the account, this may include:

  • Profile information and account identifiers
  • Personal playlists, likes, follows and account preferences
  • Artist profile details and uploaded content associated with the account, subject to a review of publication rights and obligations
  • Other information linked to the account that is no longer necessary

We will tell you if a rights, agreement or technical issue requires an additional step to remove published artist material.

Limited information we may retain

Where legally justified, limited records may be retained for security, fraud prevention, rights-management, disputes or other legal requirements. We will explain applicable retention and deletion outcomes when processing the request. We do not treat simple account suspension as deletion.

Verification and security

To prevent malicious account deletion, we may verify that you control the account. Never send your password, PIN or one-time verification code. Do not send identification documents unless our support team specifically explains why additional verification is required.

We aim to respond and process verified requests without undue delay, subject to necessary checks and applicable obligations.

Questions?

Contact support@tesohubmusic.com for assistance. For more information about data handling, read our Privacy Policy.
